# Thymic T-Cell Development: FGR Knockout vs. Wildtype Analysis

## Summary of Results
Through PAGA network modeling, ForceAtlas2 embeddings, and Diffusion Pseudotime (DPT) analysis, we evaluated the impact of fetal growth restriction (FGR) knockout mutations on mouse thymic differentiation.

### 1. Developmental Bottleneck in Knockout Phenotypes (`FDG_Genotype_Plot.png`)
* **Observation**: Wildtype cells (orange) thoroughly populate all outer terminal branches. Knockout cells (blue) are heavily restricted to the central core and intermediate transitional clusters, showing a distinct depletion at the mature endpoints.
* **Conclusion**: The knockout mutation causes a developmental arrest or severe delay, preventing thymocytes from successfully completing positive/negative selection into peripheral mature T-cells.

### 2. Transitional Dynamics & Marker Gene Expression (`FDG_Gene_Expression_Cd4.png`, `Cd8.png`)
* **Observation**: High expression of `Cd4` and `Cd8` concentrates along the shared double-positive loops before tapering off into single-positive lineages.
